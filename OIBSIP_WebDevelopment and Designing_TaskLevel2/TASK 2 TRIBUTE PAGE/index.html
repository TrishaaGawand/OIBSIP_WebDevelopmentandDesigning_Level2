About

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam was one of India's most respected scientists and the 11th President of India. Known as the "Missile Man of India", he played a major role in developing India's missile and nuclear programs. Born into a humble family, he rose through dedication and hard work to become a global icon of knowledge, leadership, and inspiration.

He worked with ISRO and DRDO, contributed to India's space and defense development, and later served as President from 2002 to 2007. Dr. Kalam loved teaching and inspiring students, encouraging them to dream big and work hard to achieve their goals.

Major Achievements

  • Known as the Missile Man of India
  • Led India's missile development programs
  • Key role in Pokhran-II nuclear tests
  • 11th President of India (2002-2007)
  • Recipient of Bharat Ratna
  • Author of "Wings of Fire"
  • Inspired millions of students worldwide

Legacy

Dr. Kalam dedicated his life to science, education, and national development. His humility, wisdom, and vision continue to inspire generations to dream big and serve the nation.
